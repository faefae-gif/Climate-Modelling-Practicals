# Climate Modelling Practicals

This repository contains a collection of climate modelling practical exercises completed as part of my postgraduate studies in Computer Science.

The practicals demonstrate the application of computational and statistical techniques to climate data analysis, including multi-model climate projections, climate trend analysis, extreme climate indices, and statistical downscaling.

## Practical Exercises

### Practical 02 — Multi-GCM Ensemble Analysis

Analysis of climate projections from multiple Global Climate Models (GCMs), focusing on ensemble-based assessment of projected climate conditions and anomalies.

**Key areas:**
- Multi-GCM climate analysis
- Climate anomalies
- Climate projection scenarios
- Ensemble analysis

**Tools:** R

---

### Practical 03 — Climate Trend Analysis

Analysis and visualization of historical climate trends using climate model data for Zambia.

**Key areas:**
- Maximum temperature trends
- Minimum temperature trends
- Precipitation trends
- Spatial climate analysis
- Climate trend mapping

**Tools:** R

---

### Practical 04 — Climate Indices

Analysis of climate extremes using climate indices derived from daily meteorological observations from Mount Makulu, Zambia.

The analysis includes indicators related to temperature extremes and precipitation characteristics.

**Selected indices include:**
- TXx — annual maximum of daily maximum temperature
- SU — summer days
- Rx1day — maximum 1-day precipitation
- R95pTOT — precipitation from very wet days

**Tools:** R

---

### Practical 05 — Statistical Downscaling

Statistical downscaling of regional climate model projections for Mount Makulu, Zambia using Empirical Quantile Mapping (EQM).

The analysis uses multiple climate models to evaluate projected mid-century climate conditions for 2041–2070 under RCP4.5 and RCP8.5.

**Key areas:**
- Bias correction
- Empirical Quantile Mapping
- Multi-model climate projections
- Maximum and minimum temperature
- Precipitation
- Mid-century climate change assessment

**Tools:** Python, Jupyter Notebook

## Data

Large raw climate datasets are not included in this repository.

The repository focuses on analysis scripts, notebooks, selected derived outputs, figures, and reports. Raw climate datasets are excluded to keep the repository lightweight and to respect the distribution requirements of the original data providers.
